The official list has no online trophies: all 55 are single-player (SixShore's trophy list; KeenGamer's roadmap). “Partner” in a description means the other protagonist, not a second player.

The road to Vice Got a Price!

Eight stages, in the order a first run meets them. Story first, because the game has no mission replay; collections and feats fill the travelling between missions; money and property next; then the 100% checklist and the platinum. Guides put the full run at roughly 200–250 hours (SixShore's roadmap), so the stages are a season, not a weekend. Two standing warnings: keep cheats off — they disable trophies (Mobalytics) — and keep a rolling manual save, because the game offers 15 manual slots and no replays (Games.gg).
